The switch

One slide locks four things.

App Store installs, adult websites, the apps you choose and the clock. Locking is instant. Try it, then slide back to see the 24 hours start.

Slide the switch to the right

Turning off

Every way out is slower than the urge.

Slide back and Detent asks why, waits ten seconds, gives you one short task, then counts down a full day. Keep protection at any step and it counts as an urge you rode out.

I have an urge

Seven tasks for the worst minutes.

None of them unlocks anything. They keep your hands busy while the wave passes, and every one counts as an urge ridden out.

Evening mode

Nothing unlocks after work.

Tell Detent when your workday ends. From then until morning there are no install windows and no turning off.

Install window

Need one app? Open a window.

Ask, wait 15 minutes, then the App Store is open for 15. After that it locks again by itself, even if you never open Detent. Social apps and adult sites stay blocked the whole time.

Progress

A slip is a gap, not a zero.

Detent shows the clean days out of your last 30 and every urge you rode out. No streak to lose. Tap a day to mark a slip.

Back door

Close the way around it.

Optional, in Settings. Detent sets your iPhone's Screen Time passcode and hides it, with a paper copy for an envelope. Seeing it again takes 72 hours.

Languages

Eight languages, switchable in the app.

Pick one in Settings. It doesn't have to match your iPhone's.
