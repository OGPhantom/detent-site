Privacy Policy

Effective 1 November 2026

In short: Detent collects no personal data. It has no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything the app knows stays on your iPhone.

Who we are

Detent is made by Nikita Storchai, an individual developer based in Ukraine. Questions about this policy: storschajnikita@gmail.com.

What Detent stores, and where

To work, Detent keeps a few things on your device only. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else.

  • Your protection settings and their timing: whether protection is on, when an unlock request or an install window started, your evening hours, the websites you added to the filter.
  • Your progress: which of the last 30 days were clean, how many times you rode out an urge, and how many unlock requests you cancelled.
  • What you write in the app: your if-then plan, your letter to yourself, and short answers such as what helped with an urge.
  • The Screen Time passcode Detent generates if you use the hard layer. It is kept in your iPhone's keychain for this device only and is not synced to iCloud. The keychain can keep it after Detent is deleted, until the iPhone is erased.

Like other app data, the first three can be included in your iPhone's own backups (iCloud or computer), under your control and Apple's privacy terms.

Screen Time

Detent uses Apple's Screen Time framework (Family Controls, Managed Settings and Device Activity) to block App Store installs, filter adult websites and shield the apps you choose. When you pick apps, categories or websites, Apple gives Detent opaque tokens instead of their names. Detent cannot see which apps you use, how long you use them or what you browse, and it never sends any Screen Time information off your device.

Motion

If you choose the walk to ride out an urge, Detent counts your steps during that walk with Apple's Core Motion, on the device. It asks for Motion & Fitness permission first, and it keeps nothing but the fact that the walk was finished.

If you choose Phone down, Detent reads the phone's orientation from its motion sensors and turns on the proximity sensor, only to tell whether the screen faces the table. It keeps none of these readings.

Camera

If you choose Follow the dot to ride out an urge, Detent can use the front (TrueDepth) camera to check that your eyes follow the dot on the screen. It asks for camera permission the first time you open that task, and the task also works if you say no.

While the dot is on screen, Apple's ARKit face tracking runs on your iPhone. From it Detent reads only which way your eyes point and whether they are open, and uses that at once to move or pause the dot. Detent takes no photos or video, keeps no image, face geometry or other face data, and sends nothing off your device. Face data is never used for advertising, marketing, identifying you or anything else. The camera stops as soon as you leave the task or the app.

Notifications

Reminders, such as an install window closing, are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. They do not go through any server.

What we share

Nothing. Detent has no third-party code that collects data, and we never sell, rent or share information about you.

Deleting your data

Deleting Detent removes its data from your iPhone, except the generated passcode in the keychain described above. Please turn protection off in Detent before deleting it, so iOS lifts its restrictions.

Children

Detent is meant for adults managing their own iPhone. It does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. If Detent ever starts collecting any data, this page will say so before the app does.
