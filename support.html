Support

Detent locks your iPhone against impulsive app installs and adult websites. Locking takes one move; unlocking takes 24 hours.

Need help? Write to storschajnikita@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model and iOS version. You will get a reply within a few days.

Requirements

An iPhone with iOS 26 or later. Detent needs Screen Time access, which you grant once when you first open it.

Questions

How do I turn protection off?

Slide the switch in Detent to request it. Protection comes off 24 hours later. You can cancel the request at any time, and every cancel is counted as a win. There is no faster way: that is the point of Detent.

I need to install an app for work or my bank.

Ask for an install window. After a 15-minute wait, installs are allowed for 15 minutes, and then the lock returns by itself. Apps you shielded stay shielded the whole time.

Why can't I ask for anything in the evening?

Evening mode runs from the end of your workday until the morning, when urges are strongest. During it, install windows and unlock requests are not available. You can change the hours in Settings; making them shorter takes effect after 24 hours.

I forgot the Screen Time passcode Detent created.

Open Detent and ask to see the code; it appears after 72 hours. You were also asked to keep a paper copy. If both are lost and you skipped the Apple Account recovery step, Apple's only way to remove a forgotten Screen Time passcode is to erase the iPhone, so please keep the paper copy safe. See Apple's guide: If you forgot your Screen Time passcode.

Detent says Screen Time access is off.

Access can be turned off in Settings › Screen Time. Open Detent and allow access again; your progress is kept.

Can Detent see my apps or my browsing?

No. Apple gives Detent anonymous tokens instead of app and website names, and nothing leaves your iPhone. Details are in the privacy policy.

How do I delete Detent?

Turn protection off in Detent first, then delete the app. Deleting it while protection is on can leave iOS restrictions in place.
